# {% include term.html key="lands_of_jail" %} - SvS Event

## Overview

Use this guide to track each day's alliance priorities during the SvS (Server vs Server) event.

## Day 1: Monday

### Priority Items

Use these items only:

- Forticlad Cores
- Construction Speed Ups (wait for SW Decree)
- Satellite Chips
- Orbit Cores
- Cosmic Cores
- Tomes

### Notes

- Save your Troop Training Speed Ups and Research Speed Ups for the specific days they will benefit most.
- Construction Duty rotates every 10 minutes. If you finish your turn early, let the team know to avoid a gap.

### Gorilla Ratio

0 / 10 / 90

## Day 2: Tuesday

### Priority Items

Use these items only:

- Forticlad Cores
- Blue Shards
- Purple Shards
- Yellow Shards
- Reins Spin for Panda (Xuanming)
- RSS Gathering (set gathering tonight so it arrives after reset tomorrow)
- Research Speed Ups

### Notes

- Save your Troop Training Speed Ups and Universal Speed Ups.
